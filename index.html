Quick start

Simulate a part in a minute

  1. Open Planform 2D. It starts with an example bracket plate.
  2. In the Simulate panel, press Pick next to Fixed and click the plate's left edge.
  3. Press Pick next to Load 1, click the big hole, and leave Fy at −1000 N.
  4. Press Run 2D check for stress colours in about a second, or Extrude & simulate 3D for a full 3D solve. Put a force in Fz to bend the plate out of plane.

The 3D solver is Python, NumPy and SciPy compiled to WebAssembly (Pyodide). It downloads about 40 MB the first time and is cached after that. Results are linear elastic: small deflections, no plasticity. Check anything that matters with a qualified engineer.
