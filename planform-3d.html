Make a solid from a 2D drawing

In Planform 2D, choose Export, then JSON, then Copy, and paste it here. Closed outlines on continuous-linetype layers become the solid. For an extrusion, shapes inside an outline become holes. For a revolve, draw half the profile to the right of the Y axis: it spins around that axis, which stands upright in 3D. Center lines, hidden lines, dimensions and text are ignored.
